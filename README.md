# Kraf Hibiscus
### Full-Stack Business Management System

**Production-Deployed Web Application | Software Engineering | Real-World Business Solution**

**Live Customer Website:** https://krafhibiscus.com

---

## 01. Project Overview

Kraf Hibiscus is a full-stack business management system developed and deployed for a Malaysian handicraft business.

The project aims to digitalise daily business operations by connecting customer-facing services with internal management workflows through a centralised web-based platform.

Rather than building a standalone e-commerce website, the system was designed to support the broader operational needs of a small business, including order processing, materials inventory, staff coordination, financial tracking, and reporting.

The platform is deployed in a production environment and continues to undergo iterative improvements based on operational requirements and testing.

## 02. The Business Problem

Small businesses often rely on manual records, spreadsheets, and disconnected communication channels to manage orders and daily operations.

This can make it difficult to maintain consistent information, monitor order progress, coordinate staff responsibilities, and generate accurate financial reports.

Kraf Hibiscus addresses these challenges by bringing related business processes together within a more structured management system.

## 03. System Overview

The platform consists of three interconnected components.

**Customer Website**

A customer-facing interface for exploring products, viewing creations, accessing account information, tracking orders, and completing supported payment workflows.

**Admin & Staff Portal**

An internal management platform covering sales and orders, creations, materials inventory, staff tasks, finance, and reporting.

**Backend API & Database**

A central service layer responsible for connecting business modules, managing authenticated access, processing operations, and maintaining data consistency.

## 04. Key Engineering Features

- Role-based access control for customer, administrative, and staff operations.
- Integrated order management and payment verification workflows.
- Materials inventory and operational record management.
- Staff task coordination and workflow tracking.
- Financial record integration and business reporting.
- Responsive customer and internal management interfaces.
- Multilingual customer-facing experience supporting English and Bahasa Melayu.

## 05. Technology Stack

**Frontend:** Vue.js, TypeScript

**Backend:** Node.js, REST APIs

**Database:** MySQL, Prisma ORM

**Deployment:** Hostinger

**File Storage:** S3-compatible object storage

**Engineering Practices:** Version control, modular development, automated testing, production builds, and deployment verification.
